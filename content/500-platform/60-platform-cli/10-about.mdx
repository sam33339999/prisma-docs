This guide demonstrates 如何 access the Prisma Data Platform using the [Prisma CLI](/orm/tools/prisma-cli). Get started by ensuring you have the Prisma CLI installed, following our setup [instructions](/orm/tools/prisma-cli#installation).

This integration, now in Early Access, expands the capabilities of the Prisma CLI, enabling management of your Platform projects directly from the command line. For a detailed overview of all the commands, please 參閱 the [Commands page](/platform/platform-cli/commands).
